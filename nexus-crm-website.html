Most implementations go live in 3–6 weeks depending on scope. A focused HubSpot setup for a 10-person sales team can be done in under 2 weeks. Enterprise Salesforce rollouts with custom objects, integrations and migration take 6–12 weeks. We always agree a timeline upfront and deliver to it.
